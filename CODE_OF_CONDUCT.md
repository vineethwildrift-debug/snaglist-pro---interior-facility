# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and users of Snaglist Pro.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward others

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Insulting or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or inflammatory comments
- Unwanted sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- Warnings
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban from the community

## Reporting

Report violations to:
- **Email:** conduct@snaglist.pro
- **GitHub:** Report via [Issues](../../issues)

---

**Be respectful. Be kind. Be inclusive.**
